About

Qinglin “Quinn” Yang (杨青林)

I am a Ph.D. student in the College of Artificial Intelligence at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Ruizhen Hu.

Before starting my Ph.D., I pursued master’s studies at Zhejiang University’s School of Software Technology, advised by Prof. Sida Peng.

I am interested in the nature of intelligence, with a focus on exploring recursive self-improvement. My earlier research spans mesh generation, metric depth estimation, and dynamic scene reconstruction.

What I work on

Looking ahead, I plan to explore recursive self-improvement (RSI) and what it can reveal about the nature of intelligence. I am particularly interested in learning and reasoning, self-reference, and the foundations of intelligence.
